Smart TTS privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Smart TTS is a speech app for blind and visually impaired people, made by Muhammad Waqas (Blind World Hub). I made it for myself and my friends first, and I treat your privacy the way I want mine treated. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your data.

The short version

  • Smart TTS never sends the text it reads aloud to the internet.
  • Smart TTS has no account, no sign up, no advertising and no analytics.
  • I never sell or share your data with anyone.
  • A problem report or a log leaves your phone only when you agree to it or send it yourself.

Speech

Smart TTS works as a speech engine for your screen reader and other apps. The text it reads is spoken by the voice engines installed on your phone, such as Speech Services by Google. Smart TTS does not send that text anywhere. Those voice engines are separate apps; some of them can use online voices, and their own privacy policies apply to them.

To make speech start faster, Smart TTS can keep the sound of a few words ready for a short time. This stays in the phone's memory only, is never saved and is never sent.

The Smart TTS helper (accessibility service)

The helper is an optional accessibility service. You turn it on yourself in Android's settings, under Accessibility, and you can turn it off there at any time. While it is on, it:

  • reads new notifications of calls, payments and verification codes, so Smart TTS can say them, also with the screen off;
  • reads the call screen while a call is ringing, only to find out who is calling;
  • reads the words of the screen in front of you, only so their sound is ready before you reach them.

The helper never presses, types or changes anything on your screen. What it reads is used only on your phone, in the moment, and is not kept or sent.

Phone calls

Announcing calls is optional and off until you turn it on. When you do:

  • the Phone permission tells Smart TTS that a call is ringing;
  • the Contacts permission is used only to find the saved name of the person who is calling;
  • on Android 10 and newer, Smart TTS can be chosen as the caller ID app (Call screening). It receives the number of an incoming call only to say who is calling. Every call rings as usual; nothing is blocked or changed.

Names and numbers are never written to a log and never sent anywhere.

Messages and polls from the developer

Now and then Smart TTS asks the Blind World Hub server whether there is a message or a poll for its users. When you answer a poll, your answer is sent together with a random number that Smart TTS made on your phone, so one phone counts once. That number is not linked to your name, your phone number or your Google account.

Problem reports

The first time something goes wrong, Smart TTS asks whether it may send problem reports. You can change your answer later in Settings. A report contains:

  • what went wrong, and the last technical lines of the app's own log;
  • the app version, the phone's make and model, and the Android version;
  • the same random number described above, so reports from one phone can be grouped.

Before a report is sent, the spoken text, names, long numbers and e-mail addresses are taken out of it. Reports travel encrypted (HTTPS) to the Blind World Hub server, are used only to find and fix problems, and are kept only as long as they are needed for that.

Log for help

"Keep a log for help" is off until you turn it on. The log stays on your phone. You decide what happens with it: share it, save it, or send it to me. If you send it, it is used only to help you, and only I can read it. You can delete the log at any time from the app.

Permissions

  • Internet: messages and polls, and problem reports or a log when you agree.
  • Notifications: messages from the developer.
  • Phone and Contacts: only for announcing calls, as described above.
  • Usage access (optional): to tell which app is on screen, for the settings you make for each app.
  • Audio settings and vibration: for sound routing, such as speech on the phone's speaker while Bluetooth plays music.

Smart TTS does not read your SMS, your files, your photos or your location, and it does not use the microphone.

Children

Smart TTS is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can turn off the helper, call announcements, problem reports and the log at any time. Uninstalling Smart TTS removes everything it kept on your phone. To have a report or a log you sent deleted, write to me and I will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with a new date at the top.
