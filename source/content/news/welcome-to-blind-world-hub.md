Blind World Hub is now online. It brings together the apps, guides and video reviews that Waqas shares for blind and visually impaired people, in one place that works well with screen readers.

## What is on the site

- **Apps:** each app has its own page with its version, a description, a download button and, where there is one, a video review.
- **Videos:** the latest videos from the Blind World Hub YouTube channel.
- **Guides:** step by step help, starting with how to install an APK file and how to check an app for viruses.

New apps and videos are added regularly. Join the [WhatsApp community](https://chat.whatsapp.com/LV2n4kBnzTy3N1zXwUiHHm) to hear about them first, and to suggest apps you would like to see here.
