title: How to change the text to speech engine on Android
description: Choose which text to speech engine your screen reader uses, for example after installing a new voice.
date: 2026-10-24
---
After you install a new voice or text to speech engine, you choose it as the engine your phone uses. The menu names differ between phone makers; the most common paths are below.

## On most phones

1. Open Settings.
2. Open Accessibility.
3. Open Text-to-speech output.
4. Press Preferred engine and choose the new engine.
5. Go back and press Play to listen to an example.

## On Samsung phones

1. Open Settings.
2. Open General management.
3. Open Language, or Language and input.
4. Open Text-to-speech.
5. Choose the new engine.

## If you cannot find the menu

Open Settings and use its search: type "text to speech" and open the result.

Some screen readers, such as Jieshuo, also have their own text to speech setting inside the screen reader's settings. Check there too.
