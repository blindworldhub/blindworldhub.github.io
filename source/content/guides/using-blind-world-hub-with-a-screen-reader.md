title: Using Blind World Hub with a screen reader
description: The fastest ways to find apps, guides and videos on Blind World Hub with TalkBack, Jieshuo, NVDA or JAWS.
date: 2026-10-24
---
Blind World Hub is built so you can move around it quickly with a screen reader.

## Move by heading

Every page has one main heading at the top of its content, and every part of a page has its own heading. On an app page these are "Download", "Video", "About this app" and "More apps".

- With TalkBack or Jieshuo, choose Headings as the reading unit and swipe up or down.
- With NVDA or JAWS on a computer, press H for the next heading, or 2 for the next part of the page.

## Skip the menu

The first link on every page is "Skip to main content". Activate it to jump past the menu straight to the content.

## Search

Every page has a search at the top. Type the name of an app or a word such as "voice", then press Search. The results page says how many results were found.

## Filter the apps

On the Apps page you can choose a category and a platform, or type a word. The page says how many apps are shown each time you change a filter.

## Display settings

The Display settings button at the top of every page opens buttons to make the text larger or smaller, switch on high contrast or the dark theme, and turn the 3D globe on or off.
