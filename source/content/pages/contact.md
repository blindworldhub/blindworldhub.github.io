The quickest way to reach Blind World Hub is the WhatsApp community. Waqas and other members read the messages there.

## WhatsApp community

[Join the Blind World Hub WhatsApp community](https://chat.whatsapp.com/LV2n4kBnzTy3N1zXwUiHHm) to ask a question, suggest an app for the site, or tell us about a broken download link.

## YouTube

[Visit the Blind World Hub YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@blindworldhub) to watch video reviews and leave a comment under any video.

## Reporting a problem with an app

If an app on this site does not work, has a problem with your screen reader, or you own an app and want it removed, send a message in the WhatsApp community with the name of the app. We will look at it as soon as possible.
