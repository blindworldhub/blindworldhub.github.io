title: Accessibility statement
description: Blind World Hub is built for screen reader users and aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.
---
Blind World Hub is made by a blind person for blind and visually impaired people. It aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## Built in

- A "Skip to main content" link at the start of every page.
- One main heading on every page and clear headings for every part, so you can move by heading with TalkBack, Jieshuo, NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver.
- Landmarks for the header, the main navigation, the search, the main content and the footer.
- Buttons and links named by what they do, for example "Download Smart Reader 2.0, APK file, 12 MB".
- A search on every page and filters on the Apps page, which tell your screen reader how many results there are.
- Display settings at the top of every page: larger or smaller text, high contrast, a dark theme, and a switch to turn off the moving 3D globe. Your choice is remembered on your device.
- No sound or video plays by itself. Moving effects stop when your device asks for reduced motion.
- Text in the Atkinson Hyperlegible font, designed by the Braille Institute for low vision readers.

## Something not working?

If a page, a button or a download does not work well with your screen reader, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact/), with the address of the page and the screen reader you use. We will fix it.
