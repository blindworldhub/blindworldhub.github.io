Blind World Hub is a home on the web for blind and visually impaired people. Here you can find apps that work well with screen readers such as TalkBack, Jieshuo, NVDA and JAWS, step by step guides, news and video reviews.

## Who runs it

Blind World Hub is run by Waqas. Waqas is blind and makes YouTube videos about apps from the Play Store, showing how each one works with a screen reader. Every app on this site is shared by Waqas, and his name and the date are shown with each upload.

## What you will find here

- Apps and downloads, each with its version, a description and a download button.
- Video reviews from the Blind World Hub YouTube channel.
- Guides that explain, step by step, how to install and use apps with a screen reader.
- News about accessibility and assistive technology.

## Built for screen readers

Every page has clear headings, a skip link, labelled buttons and a search. You can make the text larger, switch on high contrast or a dark theme, and turn off the moving 3D globe under Display settings at the top of every page. Read more in the [accessibility statement](/accessibility/).
