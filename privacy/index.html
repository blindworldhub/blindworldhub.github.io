Privacy policy

Blind World Hub does not collect, store or sell personal data. The site has no accounts, no sign up, no advertising and no tracking or analytics.

Settings on your device

If you change the text size, contrast, theme or 3D effects, your choice is saved in your own browser, on your device only, so the site remembers it next time. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP addresses of visitors for security reasons, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Downloads are served by GitHub too.

Videos

Videos come from YouTube. A video is only loaded when you press its play button, and it then plays through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to videos you play.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as the Play Store or an app developer's site. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
