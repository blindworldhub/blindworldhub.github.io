Google TTS For NVDA is now officially available in the NVDA Add-on Store

Written by Google TTS For NVDA team, shared by Waqas, November 2, 2026.

October 31 marked a truly important milestone for our development team and the community: Google TTS For NVDA has been officially approved and published in the NVDA Add-on Store (operated by NV Access). From now on, alongside major community portals and forums that have supported the project early on, such as the Official Russian NVDA Add-ons Catalog (NVDA.RU), the Blind Help Project, and many other groups and forums, users have an additional official, dependable channel right inside NVDA to install and update the add-on.

Having Google's natural, familiar voices as a synthesizer on the computer has been a long-standing wish among screen reader users for more than 10 years. In early 2025, Google released Chrome 134 with the WASM TTS Engine included as a built-in browser component. Building on that milestone, we seized the opportunity and did our very best to turn that decade-long dream into a reality for NVDA users.

Why it took until now

Many of you may wonder why it took until now for Google TTS For NVDA to appear in the Add-on Store. There are two main reasons:

  1. Including all component licenses in the installation package: By nature, the project has been legitimate from the very beginning, as it builds on open-source Chromium components and does not bundle voice packages into the installer. However, in our earliest releases, we had not yet included the license texts of the bundled components inside the installation package itself, which caused unnecessary doubts about its legal status during review. In version 1.0, all licenses for the WASM TTS Engine and related components have been added to the add-on.
  2. Overcoming opposition due to conflicting interests: Bringing a free, offline, multilingual synthesizer to the community also touched upon the interests of a certain group of people, leading to pushback aimed at keeping the add-on out of the store.

Thank you

We would like to express our sincere gratitude to all of our users. Earlier on, even though the add-on was not yet in the Add-on Store and still had quite a few bugs, you welcomed it widely, shared feedback patiently, and supported us generously so the project could grow into what it is today.

For those who still hold prejudices against this add-on, we hope to receive constructive feedback that helps us improve the experience for the community, rather than attempts to deny what has been accomplished.

The wide adoption and steady support from users, together with its official inclusion in the NVDA Add-on Store, show that Google TTS For NVDA is well worth using every day.

How to install or update from the NVDA Add-on Store

Open the NVDA menu, then Tools, then Add-on Store. For a new installation, go to the "Available add-ons" tab, search for Google TTS For NVDA, and choose "Install". When a new release becomes available, go to the "Updatable add-ons" tab and choose "Update".
