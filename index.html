  • Blind World Hub Screen Recorder

    Version 1.1

    Uploaded by Waqas

    Date: November 7, 2026

    An accessible screen recorder for blind and visually impaired people, now with background music that gets softer by itself whenever you or your screen reader speaks.

    Blind World Hub Screen Recorder is a screen recorder made for blind and visually impaired people. It works fully with TalkBack, Jieshuo and other screen readers, so you can record your phone's screen on your own: to make tutorials for YouTube, to show someone a problem on your phone, or to share how an app works with a screen reader.

    New in version 1.1: background music

    • Add background music to your recordings: choose any song or music file from your phone, and it plays softly under your recording.
    • Music volume: from silent to louder than normal. Music softer and Music louder change it while you are recording.
    • The music gets softer when someone speaks: when you or your screen reader speaks, the music goes down by itself and comes back up in the pauses, like on FM radio.
    • Ready-made styles: FM radio (recommended), YouTube tutorial, Soft and relaxed, Like GoldWave, Silent while someone speaks, My own settings, or Off.
    • Voice sensitivity: High for soft voices in a quiet room, Medium for most people, Low for noisy places, so fans and traffic do not lower the music.
    • A louder voice lowers the music a little more, so it sounds natural.
    • Clear space for the voice: only the part of the music where voices sit goes down, so the music stays full and your voice stays clear.
    • Every song at about the same loudness, so every video sounds even.
    • The music comes in gently over 2 seconds and fades out at the end.
    • Background music in Make video: add music to a recording you already made, with the same options.

    Why we made it

    Most screen recorders are hard to use without sight: unlabeled buttons, no clear signal when recording starts, and the screen reader's voice is often missing from the video. This app was built so that a blind person knows exactly what is happening at every moment.

    Main features

    • Every button and setting is labeled and speaks clearly with any screen reader.
    • Countdown before recording (you choose the seconds), spoken aloud and felt with a vibration for each second; a long vibration tells you recording has started. Nothing is spoken after that, so your recording stays clean.
    • Record your own voice and the screen reader's voice together through the microphone, whatever voice engine you use. On Android 10 or newer, the phone's internal sound can be recorded too, where the phone allows it.
    • Pause, Resume and Stop and save from the notification, from the app, from the quick settings button, or from floating Pause and Stop buttons at the side of the screen.
    • Add a Record button to quick settings, to start recording from anywhere.
    • Take a screenshot with one button.
    • Video quality: 360p, 480p, 720p (recommended), 1080p, or the phone's own screen size; 15, 24, 30 or 60 frames a second; follow the phone's rotation or always portrait or landscape.
    • Optional: stop and save automatically when the screen is locked, speak when a recording is saved, vibrate for start, pause and save.
    • Recordings are saved in the Movies folder and screenshots in the Pictures folder. From the Recordings list you can share, rename or delete them.
    • Automatic update notice: when a new version is published, the app tells you and opens the download.
    • A simple error log you can share to help us fix problems. It never contains your recordings or personal data.

    Privacy

    Your recordings and your music stay on your phone until you share them. The app sends nothing about you anywhere; it only checks for a new version.

    Works on Android 7 and newer. Free, with no ads.

    Developer: Muhammad Waqas Farooq, Blind World Hub.

  • Smart TTS

    Version 1.3

    Uploaded by Waqas

    Date: November 4, 2026

    A multilingual text-to-speech engine for screen reader users: every language is read in its own voice, automatically, with a fast built-in English voice and many reading options.

    Smart TTS is a text-to-speech engine for Android, made for screen reader users (TalkBack, Jieshuo). It reads every part of the text in its own language and voice by itself: Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and English, even mixed in one sentence.

    Features

    • 205 languages; choose any installed voice for each language.
    • Built-in English voice Reed (Eloquence): fast, clear, works offline, with sound profiles, voice shape and pronunciation dictionaries.
    • Reading options: short web links, split joined words, Roman numerals, emojis in the language of the text, clear e-mail addresses.
    • Numbers: verification codes digit by digit, Urdu and Hindi numbers in words, lakh and crore.
    • NEW: codes from SMS are copied and said slowly, wherever you are.
    • Make audio file from any text, with its own speed.
    • Audio optimization, audio routing and low latency mode.
    • Keep voices running: shortcuts to battery and background settings.
    • Fully accessible, with a guide in English and Urdu, and automatic update notices.

    Your text, codes and SMS never leave your phone. Android 7 and newer. Free, no ads.

  • Google Hindi voice for MultiTTS

    Version 1.0

    Uploaded by Waqas

    Date: November 1, 2026

    Google's Hindi voice pack for MultiTTS: import this zip file into MultiTTS to read Hindi with a clear, natural Google voice.

    This is a Google Hindi voice pack for MultiTTS. It lets MultiTTS read Hindi with a clear and natural Google voice, for TalkBack, Jieshuo, Smart TTS and any app that uses text to speech.

    How to install

    1. Install MultiTTS first (you can download it from Blind World Hub).
    2. Download this zip file. Do not unzip it.
    3. Open MultiTTS and choose Import voice pack.
    4. Select this zip file and wait until the import is finished.
    5. Choose the Hindi speaker from this pack.
    6. To use it with your screen reader, go to Settings, Accessibility, Text-to-speech output, and select MultiTTS. Or choose MultiTTS as the Hindi voice in Smart TTS.

    Good to know

    The voice works offline after it is imported.

    You can change the speed and pitch in MultiTTS.

  • Multi TTS

    Version 1.8.3

    Uploaded by Waqas

    Date: October 26, 2026

    MultiTTS is a free text-to-speech engine for Android with no voices of its own: you import voice packs (zip files) for any language, choose the speaker you like, and use it with TalkBack, Jieshuo and other screen readers.

    MultiTTS is a free text-to-speech (TTS) engine for Android. It comes with no voices of its own. Instead, you import the voices you want into it, as voice pack zip files, and then use them on your phone.

    How it works

    Import a voice pack: download a voice pack zip file, then import it into MultiTTS.

    Choose a speaker: after importing, choose the speaker (voice) you like from that pack.

    Use it everywhere: MultiTTS works as your phone's text-to-speech engine, so screen readers such as TalkBack and Jieshuo, e-book readers and other apps can speak with the voice you chose.

    Main features

    Voice packs are available for many languages of the world, not only a few.

    Import as many voice packs as you want, and change the speaker at any time.

    Choose a different voice for each language, and change the speed and pitch to suit you.

    How to start

    1. Download the APK below and install it.
    2. Open MultiTTS and import a voice pack zip file.
    3. Choose the speaker you want to use.
    4. Go to your phone's Settings, then Accessibility, then Text-to-speech output, and select MultiTTS as the preferred engine.

    MultiTTS is made by its own developer and shared here for the convenience of blind and visually impaired users.
