About this app

Smart TTS is a multilingual text-to-speech engine for Android, created by Waqas of Blind World Hub for blind and visually impaired people who read in more than one language every day. A single message can mix two languages, a news article can move between scripts, and a phone menu can change language in the middle of a line. Ordinary speech engines read one language well and stumble over the rest. Smart TTS was built to solve this problem: it recognises each language as it reads, even inside one sentence, and speaks it with its own proper voice, switching automatically and smoothly, so that whatever appears on your screen is heard correctly without changing settings or switching engines again and again. It was designed and tested by a blind user, for blind users. Each language is spoken by the voice you choose for it, using the voice engines already installed on your phone, and Smart TTS includes Reed, a crisp and responsive built-in English voice that stays clear even at high speed. It supports English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Persian, Bengali, Punjabi, Tamil, French, German, Spanish and many more languages, with smooth, high-quality joins between voices and no clicks, gaps or jumps in loudness. You can choose Mixed mode, where every language in the text is read with its own voice, or Dual languages mode, where you pick your two main languages and decide whether other languages are read or skipped. Every voice is fully under your control. Each language has its own speed, pitch and volume, set with sliders or with Decrease and Increase buttons, and the volume keys adjust whichever slider your screen reader is on. Volume can go above 100 percent, protected by a limiter so the sound never cracks, and equal loudness keeps all voices balanced. Speech follows your screen reader's own speed, including for notifications and system announcements, and Reed offers three clarity levels: 11 kHz classic, 16 kHz clear and 22 kHz clearest. Smart TTS also reads more intelligently. Phone numbers can be read as digits, pairs or triplets, while times, prices and years are read naturally. Arabic, Urdu and Hindi digits are read as proper numbers, and common abbreviations such as Dr., Wed and Aug are read in full. Punctuation can be set to None, Some, Most or All, signs are named when you move through text character by character, and e-mail and web addresses are always read correctly. A pronunciation dictionary lets you add your own words and names for each voice or language, and sentence pauses adjust to your reading speed. It is fast and dependable. Voices are prepared in advance so speech starts quickly, even for the first sentence in a new language, and they are kept awake so they never fall silent. If a voice does not respond, Smart TTS asks it again instead of going quiet. A built-in error log and an optional sound log make it easy to report problems, and Smart TTS never uses the microphone. The app itself is simple and fully accessible with TalkBack, Jieshuo and other Android screen readers. Its home screen has clear pages for Modes, Languages, Voices, Settings, and About and guide, one button sets Smart TTS as your default engine, and a search box in the Languages page finds any language instantly. It works on Android 7 up to Android 16, on both 32-bit and 64-bit phones. Smart TTS brings all your languages together in one engine, so you can read your messages, news, books and apps the way they were written, clearly and comfortably.
