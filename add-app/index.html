On the GitHub page

  1. Everything you typed is already filled in. Do not change the tag.
  2. Find the button "Attach binaries by dropping them here or selecting them" and choose the APK or other file. Skip this for a Play Store app without a file.
  3. Wait until the file has finished uploading, then press "Publish release".
  4. About two minutes later the app has its own page here, with your name and a download button.
